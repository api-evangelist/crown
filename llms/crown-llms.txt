# Crown Holdings, Inc.

> Crown is a leading global supplier of rigid packaging products to consumer marketing companies, as well as transit and protective packaging products, equipment and services to a broad range of end markets.

## About This Site
Crown Holdings, Inc. (NYSE: CCK) is a worldwide leader in metal packaging with operations in 39 countries. Founded in 1892, Crown manufactures aluminum beverage cans, food cans and ends, aerosol containers, metal closures, promotional packaging, and transit packaging solutions. With 189 manufacturing plants and sales facilities globally and approximately 23,000 employees, Crown delivers brand-building packaging solutions that help customers connect with consumers while advancing sustainability through its Twentyby30 program.
